The science

Your body on laughter

Researchers have been taking laughter apart for decades. What they found is that a laugh is a precise, rhythmic, very old instrument, and that playing it changes you.

gelotology
/ˌdʒel.əˈtɒl.ə.dʒi/  noun
  1. The study of laughter and its effects on the body and mind.
  2. A field whose findings mostly agree with your grandmother.

From Greek gelōs, “laughter.” The word for the study of laughter came into use in the 1960s. Laughter itself is several million years older.

Anatomy of a “ha”

Shape a laugh, then play it.

The defaults follow Robert Provine’s measurements: each “ha” lasts about 75 milliseconds, notes repeat about every 210 milliseconds, and a laugh fades as it goes. We almost never laugh in the middle of a sentence. Laughter lands at the pauses, like punctuation.

30×

It’s a social signal

People are about thirty times more likely to laugh with others than alone. Most laughter follows ordinary remarks, not jokes.

Provine, Laughter: A Scientific Investigation, 2000
Opioids

It dulls pain

After bouts of social laughter, people tolerate more pain, and brain scans show the body’s own opioids being released.

Dunbar et al., Proc. R. Soc. B, 2012 · Manninen et al., J. Neurosci., 2017
Wider

It relaxes blood vessels

Watching a funny film widened the lining of volunteers’ blood vessels. A stressful film made them narrow.

Miller & Fry, Medical Hypotheses, 2009
10M+

It’s older than words

Great apes laugh when tickled and during play. The sound traces back to a common ancestor more than 10 million years ago.

Davila-Ross et al., Current Biology, 2009

Contagion

Catch it

Hearing laughter primes the parts of your brain that move your face into a smile. That is why one laugh can travel around a whole room.

Tap anyone to start them laughing, then watch it spread to the people nearby.

Practice

One minute of laughter yoga

Laughter yoga began in a Mumbai park in 1995. Its premise is simple: start with pretend laughter, and it soon turns real, especially in a group. Stand up, press start, and follow along.
